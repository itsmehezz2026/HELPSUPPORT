QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

What happens after I submit a report?＋

In a live service, your report should be securely recorded and reviewed by an authorized moderation team. This demo does not send reports anywhere yet.

Can I report something that happened in a private message?＋

A production site can provide a private-message reporting flow. Include only the information needed for the safety team to understand the concern.

How can I protect my account?＋

Use a unique password, turn on two-factor authentication when available, avoid suspicious links, and never share verification codes.

How do I contact support?＋

Use the live chat bubble on this page or the “Chat with support” button to message our support team.
